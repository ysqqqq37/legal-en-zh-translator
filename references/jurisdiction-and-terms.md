# Legal English ↔ Chinese: compact runtime references

Checked 22 October 2026. These are translation safeguards, not an opinion on enforceability. Chinese rendering candidates below are translator suggestions, not quotations from a statutory bilingual text or asserted legal equivalents. Identify governing law, forum, document type, target readership, and simplified/traditional script independently. “UK” is not a single contract-law jurisdiction; “US law” often requires a state and sometimes federal-law identification. Preserve unresolved ambiguity and flag it outside the translated text.

1. **Consideration: 对价 / 代价, context-dependent.** Distinguish the common-law technical concept from a particular purchase price or payment amount; do not replace every instance with 价款 or translate ordinary “in consideration of” mechanically. Do not insert a consideration recital absent from the Chinese source or decide that a recital validates a contract. Hong Kong Land Registry guidance illustrates transaction-specific monetary use, not a universal definition: https://www.landreg.gov.hk/en/form/consideration.htm . For an E&W consideration issue, consult the judgment, not an inferred slogan: *Rock Advertising v MWB*, [2018] UKSC 24, paragraph 18 (the consideration question was not decided): https://supremecourt.uk/cases/uksc-2016-0152 .

2. **Representations and warranties: 陈述与保证, as a provisional pair.** Keep the two source concepts distinct; do not collapse them to 承诺, or treat warranty as a third-party guarantee/保证担保. Preserve qualifiers, knowledge standards, disclosure exceptions, survival and remedy provisions. Misrepresentation remedies and contractual warranty remedies cannot be inferred from the Chinese label alone. E&W reference: Misrepresentation Act 1967, especially sections 1–3: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1967/7/contents . For a context-specific illustration that contractual labeling is not decisive, Sale of Goods Act 1979 s11; check the current version and applicable transaction: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1979/54/contents . Do not export these rules wholesale to US state law.

3. **Indemnity / indemnify: 弥偿、赔偿 or 补偿 according to audience and clause.** Preserve each loss category, trigger, beneficiary, causation connector, first-/third-party scope, defense/control obligations, exclusions and limits. Translate “defend,” “indemnify,” and “hold harmless” separately where the source separates them; do not add a duty to defend or promise unlimited reimbursement. *Wood v Capita*, [2017] UKSC 24, illustrates why exact clause triggers and the contract as a whole matter: https://supremecourt.uk/cases/uksc-2015-0212 ; official explanatory summary: https://supremecourt.uk/uploads/uksc_2015_0212_press_summary_ab8ca94f16.pdf .

4. **Best/reasonable/commercially reasonable efforts or endeavours.** Candidate wording includes 尽最大努力 / 尽合理努力 / 尽商业上合理的努力, but preserve every modifier and the defined performance target. Never announce a universal ranked ladder or convert an efforts obligation into a guaranteed result. Delaware Chancery's *AB Stable VIII LLC v MAPS Hotels*, C.A. 2020-0310-JTL (30 Nov 2020), printed pp207–210, discusses limits of the supposed hierarchy and Delaware cases applying contextual reasonableness: https://courts.delaware.gov/Opinions/Download.aspx?id=313600 . This is Delaware authority, not a rule for all US states or E&W.

5. **Shall / may / must.** Translate function, not the token: obligation (应/应当/须), discretion or permission (可/有权), prohibition (不得), future or declaration as appropriate. Preserve conditions and negation; “may not” can need an ambiguity flag. Never mechanically upgrade “may” to obligation or translate all “shall” as 将. The UK OPC drafting guidance avoids legislative shall, but explicitly is not a guide to statutory interpretation or past drafting practice; do not misuse it to rewrite existing contracts: https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/drafting-bills-for-parliament/2024-03-19-drafting-guidance (1.2.9 and introduction).

6. **Without prejudice.** In a rights-saving clause, 不影响… can be apt; as a settlement heading retain “Without prejudice” alongside a carefully qualified Chinese rendering such as 不损害权利, and flag its procedural context. Never promise blanket confidentiality, privilege, or inadmissibility from the label alone. E&W: *Oceanbulk v TMT*, [2010] UKSC 44, recognizes exceptions: https://www.supremecourt.uk/cases/uksc-2010-0039 . US federal evidence rule 408 is a separate, purpose-limited regime, not a translation equivalent for all jurisdictions: https://www.uscourts.gov/forms-rules/current-rules-practice-procedure/federal-rules-evidence . Check the current PDF and any relevant state rule.

7. **Deed: 契据, with the original term retained when helpful.** Do not flatten to ordinary 合同 or assume a US real-property deed and an E&W deed have identical functions. Preserve execution, attestation, delivery, capacity and signature blocks; do not invent seals or notarization. For individuals in E&W, Law of Property (Miscellaneous Provisions) Act 1989 s1 specifies intended deed status, execution and delivery, and does not require an individual's seal: https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/1989/34/section/1 . Corporate execution needs separate applicable rules; this source is not a US formalities checklist.

8. **Equitable relief/remedies: 衡平法救济 when denoting the technical legal category, not simply 公平救济.** Distinguish technical equity terminology from an evaluative use of “equitable” meaning fair or just in its particular statutory or contractual context. Do not infer a technical equity test from that adjective alone, or conversely flatten every technical use into ordinary fairness. Consult matching authoritative bilingual wording when available and disclose a material unresolved choice rather than asserting one universal equivalent. Preserve the distinction between seeking relief and entitlement to receive it. Specific performance (特定履行/强制实际履行, audience-dependent), injunction and damages are not interchangeable. Do not imply that translating a contractual acknowledgment of irreparable harm guarantees a court order. *eBay v MercExchange*, 547 US 388 (2006), explains equitable discretion and a four-factor test in the US federal permanent-injunction/patent context, not an all-remedies universal test: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USREPORTS-547/pdf/USREPORTS-547-388.pdf .

## Bilingual lookup discipline

Hong Kong DOJ Combined Glossaries: https://www.glossary.doj.gov.hk/ . Useful terminology evidence for Hong Kong legal Chinese, not proof of mainland-PRC legal equivalence. Check the selected division/source and surrounding instrument. Script conversion is not jurisdiction conversion. The site warns that automated simplified-character content is not equivalent to its published traditional content. Do not scrape or reproduce the glossary wholesale; its current terms specifically address mass replication/product reproduction. The candidates above are independent translator suggestions, not claimed copies of verified individual DOJ entries.

## Safe runtime research

Use only public term, statute and case queries. Do not paste client names, facts, clauses or documents into external search or translation services without authorization. Recheck current law only when the actual task requires legal-effect explanation; use the source jurisdiction's official text, date/version and scope, and clearly separate legal analysis from faithful translation. No source here establishes identical effect between mainland Chinese law, Hong Kong law, E&W law and US state/federal law.
